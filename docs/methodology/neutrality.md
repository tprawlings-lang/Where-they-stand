# Neutrality

Where They Stand reports evidence-backed candidate positions without endorsing candidates, parties, or policies.

- Party affiliation cannot establish or change a stance.
- Lack of evidence is not opposition. A completed search without qualifying evidence produces No Public Position Found.
- Every candidate is evaluated against the same version of the same canonical question.
- Supports and Opposes receive equal space, typography, and visual weight.
- Candidate ordering uses official ballot order when available, then a disclosed alphabetical rule.
- AI may propose evidence and a label, but a human reviewer must approve any public political classification.
